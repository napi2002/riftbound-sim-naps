How to play
  • First to 8 points wins. Conquer a battlefield (be the only one there after moving in) for 1, and score 1 for each battlefield you hold at the start of your turn.
  • Your final point can't come from a conquer unless you've already scored the other battlefield this turn; you draw 1 instead.
  • Each turn: ready everything, score holds, channel 2 runes, draw 1, then play cards and move.
  • Runes pay for cards: exhaust one for 1 energy, recycle one of the card's domain for 1 power. Payment is automatic.
  • Units enter exhausted in your base. Tap ready units, then a Move button, to send them base ⇄ battlefield. Moving several together is one attack.
  • Combat: each side deals its total Might. Tank units take damage first, Backline last. If only attackers survive they conquer; if both sides survive the attackers go home.
  • Every rules line is marked Simulated or Not simulated; unsimulated text is ignored.
