# Suggested decklists (Nov 2026, legal from the Nov 23 2026 bans)

The game's suggested decks. Source: riftbound.gg tournament lists (Radiance). Banned cards (Riven, Broken Blade; Zhonya's Hourglass; Stellacorn Herder; Nocturne, Horrifying; Rhasa the Sunderer; Brynhir Thundersong; Dazzling Aurora; Baited Hook; Emperor's Dais; Star Spring) were swapped for the most-played legal cards of the same legend. Where a legend's newest list relies on cards the simulator can't play yet, the previous researched list is kept (with the same ban swaps).
